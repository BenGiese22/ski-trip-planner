Where to go

Skiing is one part of the weekend, not the whole point. Each option below is the mountain, the town, the food, and what else there is to do.

Steamboat Springs
Leaning toward this
≈3h15 drive from DEN
$900–1,300 est. per person
Direct flights from SFO + ORD this winter

On the mountain

  • Champagne powder, gentle green terrain low on the hill
  • More pitch up top if a few of you want it
  • Lift reservations required on the Ikon network here

Getting around

  • Free in-town bus (the SST) links downtown to the gondola
  • A car is optional if you're staying central

Food, drink & town

  • Walkable historic downtown along Lincoln Ave
  • A cluster of breweries within a few blocks of each other
  • Specific restaurant picks — still need real research

Off the snow

  • Old Town Hot Springs — developed, family-friendly
  • Strawberry Park Hot Springs — rustic, natural pools outside town
  • Shops along Lincoln Ave downtown
Sources: steamboat.com — getting here, ikonpass.com — access details
Summit County — Frisco, Dillon, Silverthorne
Best value
≈1h45 drive from DEN
$650–950 est. per person
Skis Copper Mountain

On the mountain

  • Terrain zoned left to right by ability
  • Mixed group regroups easily at the same base area
  • Unlimited on the Ikon network, no blackout dates

Getting around

  • Three towns spread along I-70
  • Free Summit Stage bus connects them, or bring a car

Food, drink & town

  • Frisco's Main Street is the most walkable of the three
  • Silverthorne/Dillon lean toward strip-mall convenience
  • Specific restaurant picks — still need real research

Off the snow

  • Outlets at Silverthorne for shopping
  • Dillon Reservoir for a scenic walk
  • Cheapest condo/Airbnb inventory of the three towns
Sources: ikonpass.com — Copper Mountain access, summitstage.com
Winter Park
Closest to Denver
≈1h20 drive from DEN
$700–1,000 est. per person
No I-70 — via US-40 / Berthoud Pass

On the mountain

  • Biggest terrain pool of the three
  • Unlimited on the Ikon network
  • Mary Jane side has real bumps and glades if anyone wants more

Getting around

  • Small enough to walk most of downtown
  • A car helps for restaurant-hopping to nearby Fraser

Food, drink & town

  • Smaller, lower-key main street than the other two
  • Fewer options, but easy to get a table
  • Specific restaurant picks — still need real research

Off the snow

  • Fraser Tubing Hill for anyone sitting out a ski day
  • Low-key, farmers-market feel rather than resort-village feel
Sources: ikonpass.com — Winter Park access, winterparkresort.com
